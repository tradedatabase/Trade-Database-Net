# Sample trade datasets

Real, record-level **sample** exports from [tradedatabase.net](https://tradedatabase.net) —
bill of lading, customs and company data. These are **teaser samples**: company
names, products, HS codes, values, ports and dates are all included, while the
direct-contact fields (`phone`, `email/contact`, `tax_id`, street `address`) are
masked as `[in full dataset]`.

The full datasets — with the complete importer & exporter contacts for **195+
countries, 2015–2026** — are available at **[tradedatabase.net](https://tradedatabase.net)**
([free sample on request](https://tradedatabase.net/sample)).

| File | What it is | Rows |
|------|------------|------|
| [india-import-bill-of-lading-sample.csv](india-import-bill-of-lading-sample.csv) | India import bill of lading shipments (with ITC-HS 8-digit) | 1,000 |
| [netherlands-bill-of-lading-sample.csv](netherlands-bill-of-lading-sample.csv) | Netherlands export & import bill of lading shipments (CN8) | 1,000 |
| [nigeria-customs-data-sample.csv](nigeria-customs-data-sample.csv) | Nigeria customs declarations (CET-10, duty, tax, CIF/FOB) | 1,000 |
| [india-netherlands-spices-trade-sample.csv](india-netherlands-spices-trade-sample.csv) | India→Netherlands spices trade lane | 200 |
| [south-africa-vehicle-importers-sample.csv](south-africa-vehicle-importers-sample.csv) | South Africa motorcycle & vehicle importers | 200 |
| [global-company-master-sample.csv](global-company-master-sample.csv) | Company master records (name, location, industry) | 1,040 |

See [`../data/bill-of-lading-data-dictionary.csv`](../data/bill-of-lading-data-dictionary.csv)
for what every field means.
